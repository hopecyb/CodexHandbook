Whenever a conclusion depends on external facts, ask for the sources as well as the conclusion.

Ask the agent:

```text
Attach a source URL or reference to each key fact. Clearly mark anything uncertain as “unverified.”
```

Then open the links and check them yourself. For search features, see [Web search](/en/guide/tools/web-search/).

## Common misunderstandings

### 1. A confident tone does not make a source reliable

Fluent, confident wording and factual reliability are different things.

### 2. Must every sentence have a source?

The following especially deserve sources:

- Key facts
- Numbers that affect a decision
- Dates, policies, and product differences
- Claims you intend to use publicly

### 3. A link still needs checking

Open it and confirm at least:

- Does it actually support this conclusion?
- Is it a source you trust?
- Is the information outdated?

In research tasks, the sources themselves are part of how you judge a conclusion's credibility.
