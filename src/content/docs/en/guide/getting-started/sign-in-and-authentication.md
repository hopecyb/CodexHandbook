Sign in through your chosen client per on-screen prompts. You may see:

- Browser redirect authorization
- SSO / organization account
- Device code or Token (CLI scenarios)

## After sign-in, confirm

- The displayed account or organization is yours
- You can create or open a project
- No errors like "no permission to use Codex"

Authentication details and security guidance follow official docs: [https://developers.openai.com/codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex). On failure, see the [Troubleshooting index](/guide/reference/troubleshooting/).
