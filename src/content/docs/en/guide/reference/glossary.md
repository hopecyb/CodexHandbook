## How to use it

This is a quick-reference dictionary, not a full tutorial. When a term in another page escapes you, look it up here and return to what you were reading.

If most terms are unfamiliar, start with [Terminology for Beginners](/en/guide/start-here/terminology-for-beginners/).

## Common misconceptions

### 1. You do not need to memorize it from start to finish

A typical use is to encounter a word elsewhere, check it here, and then continue with the original page.

### 2. Understanding a short definition does not mean you know how to use it

The glossary helps identify the idea. For actual usage, read its topic chapter and examples.

### 3. Many new terms do not mean you cannot keep learning

Codex has some unfamiliar vocabulary. Learn the rough meaning first and fill in details gradually.

## When you encounter an unfamiliar term

1. Look up the short definition.
2. Identify the broad kind of concept.
3. Open the relevant topic page for a full explanation.

That keeps one word from interrupting your reading for too long.
