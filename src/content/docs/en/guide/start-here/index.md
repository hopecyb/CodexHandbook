This handbook is primarily for **AI beginners**. It does not assume you know the terminal, Git, or agents, but it introduces the capabilities you will encounter step by step.

## What you will learn

- What Codex can and cannot do
- How to choose a reading route by role and task
- How to complete a first reviewable task in a safe practice project
- How to write prompts with “goal, constraints, acceptance” and develop a review habit

## Suggested reading order

1. [What is Codex?](/en/guide/start-here/what-is-codex/) — understand its abilities and limits
2. [Choose a client](/en/guide/choose-your-client/) — choose among App, CLI, IDE, and Cloud
3. [Complete your first task](/en/cases/first-task/) — make a small change with a clear prompt and review it
4. [Permissions and sandbox](/en/guide/permissions-and-sandbox/) — understand approval, isolation, and network boundaries
5. [Context and files](/en/guide/context-and-files/) — provide useful input
6. [Verify results](/en/guide/verification/) — build review and testing habits

## Common misconceptions

### 1. “Start here” will not cover everything at once

This chapter clarifies where to begin and in what order. It does not try to teach everything on one page.

### 2. You can continue even if many terms are unfamiliar

First, understand:

- How Codex collaborates
- How to begin a task
- How to inspect results

Learn the other details while using it.

### 3. Understanding concepts alone is not enough to get started

Combine understanding with small-scope practice and review of the results.

## The first 30 minutes

| Time | What to do |
|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Read the safety points in [Permissions and sandbox](/en/guide/permissions-and-sandbox/) |
| 5–10 min | Install and sign in to your chosen client (for example, the [desktop App](/en/guide/desktop-app/)) |
| 10–25 min | Complete the [first-task example](/en/cases/first-task/) |
| 25–30 min | Browse [Prompt basics](/en/prompts/basics/) and prepare to write clearer tasks |

If you get stuck, see [Troubleshooting](/en/guide/troubleshooting/).
