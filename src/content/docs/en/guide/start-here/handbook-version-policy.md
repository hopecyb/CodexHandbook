## Ongoing updates

This handbook is not frozen indefinitely to a single Codex version. The product continues to change, so installation, sign-in, permissions, models, configuration, and Cloud behavior may change over time.

## What to trust

For current product behavior, rely on [OpenAI's official Codex documentation](https://developers.openai.com/codex). This handbook explains concepts, maps useful paths, and provides practical methods. If the two conflict, follow the official documentation and consider reporting the issue through the correction link at the bottom of the page.

## How to judge whether content still applies

For information that may change, consider three things:

1. Whether the topic changes frequently, such as installation, configuration, permissions, or product entry points.
2. Whether the visible content update date is far in the past.
3. Whether the cited official sources still match the current product behavior.

Concepts, methods, and acceptance practices are usually more stable than exact buttons, parameters, and interface paths. For current behavior, parameters, pricing, or rules, check the official sources again.

## Content updates and corrections

[Content updates and corrections](/en/guide/updates/) records important changes and their effects. The page footer can open a GitHub correction draft with the current page title and path. Review it and remove sensitive information before submitting.

## Topics that change often

- Installation methods and system requirements
- Sign-in, account, and permission flows
- Models, configuration options, and parameter syntax
- Cloud, client, and product entry-point behavior
- Pricing, quotas, and service limits
