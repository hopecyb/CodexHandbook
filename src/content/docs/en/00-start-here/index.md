Welcome to the Codex Handbook. This handbook assumes **AI beginners** as the default reader: we don't assume you know the terminal, Git, or Agents, but we will build professional skills step by step.

## What you'll get

- Clarify what Codex can and cannot do
- Choose a reading path by role and task
- Complete your first verifiable task in a safe practice project
- Learn to write prompts with goals, constraints, and acceptance criteria—and build a review habit

## Suggested reading order

1. [What is Codex](/00-start-here/what-is-codex/)
2. [Who is this handbook for](/00-start-here/who-is-this-handbook-for/)
3. [Choose your path](/00-start-here/choose-your-path/)
4. Continue to [Foundations](/01-foundations/) and [Getting Started](/02-getting-started/)

If you want to jump in right away, go straight to [First 30 minutes](/02-getting-started/first-30-minutes/), and skim the [Beginner safety checklist](/02-getting-started/beginner-safety-checklist/) first.
