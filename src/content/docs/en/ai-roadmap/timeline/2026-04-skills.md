## What problem it addresses

A Skill organizes a repeatable task's methods and resources into a reusable package. It may include SKILL.md, references, scripts, and examples. After loading its instructions, model judgment, tool execution, and result review are still required.

## Learning advice and scope

The following is this site's teaching judgment, not a measurement of industry adoption or effectiveness:

Extract a Skill from a method that has already been verified once, then test it with positive and negative trigger examples, unusual inputs, and quality checks. Do not confuse installability or passing structural checks with correct triggering in a client. The 2026-04 segment in the original URL remains for compatibility and no longer represents the historical event date.

## Hands-on exercise

[Four types of Skill download packages](/en/skills/examples/): choose a writing, research, review, or automation package, read its boundaries, run its checks manually, and record whether the client triggers it correctly. Script tests cannot replace evaluation of model routing and semantic quality.
