## Verifiable event and scope

**Date: 2020-05-22 · Type: paper publication.** Lewis and colleagues submitted v1 of Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks to arXiv. This records the date of that paper; it does not claim that combining retrieval and generation was previously nonexistent.

Source: [Original RAG paper and submission history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401). Checked on 2026-09-18; the source publication date and this page’s check date mean different things.

## What problem it addresses

RAG uses retrieved external material in generation. In practice, check separately whether retrieval found relevant material, whether that material is reliable, and whether it supports the answer.

## Learning advice and limits of applicability

The following is this site’s teaching judgment, not a measure of industry adoption or effectiveness:

First make conclusions traceable to specific evidence, then consider expanding the index and retrieval pipeline. Adding a link does not make a citation correct; preserve conflicts when sources disagree. The old page organized its content around a 2023 product event.
