## What problem it addresses

A prompt gives the model the goal, necessary context, constraints, and output requirements. Examples can clarify expectations, but greater length does not guarantee better results.

## Learning advice and limits

The following is this site’s teaching judgment, not a measure of industry adoption or effectiveness:

State the deliverable first, then add information the model lacks. Execute simple tasks directly; clarify key unknowns first. Do not treat writing a plan before every task or waiting for approval at every step as a universal rule.
