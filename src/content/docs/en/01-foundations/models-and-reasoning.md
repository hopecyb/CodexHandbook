Available models and "reasoning / thinking" tiers change with product updates. Balance:

- **Task difficulty**: simple rewrite vs multi-file architecture decisions
- **Latency and cost**: higher reasoning strength is usually slower and more expensive
- **Verifiability**: harder tasks need explicit plans and tests

This handbook doesn't lock to a specific model marketing name. Use your client's current list and [https://developers.openai.com/codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex).
