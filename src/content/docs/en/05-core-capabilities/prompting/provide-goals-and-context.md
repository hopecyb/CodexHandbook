**Goal** describes the final deliverable, not a vague wish.  
"Make the website better" → "Change the homepage primary button copy to … and keep existing style class names."

**Background** adds what the reader knows but the Agent needs: tech stack, audience, deadline reasons, known pitfalls.

Related: project-level background belongs in [Project context](/05-core-capabilities/context/project-context/)—avoid repeating long background in every prompt.
