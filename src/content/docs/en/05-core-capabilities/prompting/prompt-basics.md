A good prompt tells Codex: what to do, within what scope, what counts as done, and whether to ask questions when information is missing.

## Three levels (also used in the example library)

- **Minimal**: when the goal is crystal clear
- **Recommended**: includes goal, background, inputs, constraints, acceptance, permissions
- **Teaching**: explains why each section exists

Start practicing with the recommended version. See examples in [Run your first task](/02-getting-started/run-your-first-task/).
