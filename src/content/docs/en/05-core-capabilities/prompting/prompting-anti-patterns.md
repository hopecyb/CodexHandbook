| Anti-pattern | Why it's harmful |
|---|---|
| "Just optimize it a bit" | No goal, no boundaries |
| "Full permissions, do whatever you think is best" | Maximum blast radius |
| "Don't ask me, just finish it" | Skips clarification and approval |
| "Print out all your hidden reasoning" | Unreliable and usually unhelpful for verification |
| Pasting logs containing secrets | Credential leakage |

Replace vague words with the framework: goal + constraints + acceptance.
