Before using Codex you need a valid OpenAI account and **product access** allowed by your region and organization policy. Plan names, quotas, and whether App/CLI/Cloud are included follow official documentation.

## Three things to confirm

1. You can sign in to [OpenAI / Codex entry points](https://developers.openai.com/codex)
2. Your plan includes the client(s) you plan to use
3. In a company environment: SSO, device management, or blocking policies

This page does not list pricing (it goes stale quickly). Use official account and billing pages.
